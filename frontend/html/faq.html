Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how Flight Sight helps travelers compare flights using more than just price. Our platform is designed to make it easier to book flights with safety, emissions, and environmental impact in mind.

Why use Flight Sight?

Flight Sight helps travelers make smarter decisions by combining flight search with extra insights like airline safety ratings, emissions data, and broader environmental considerations. We believe booking a flight should be about more than just finding the cheapest option.

What is Flight Sight?

Flight Sight is a flight-booking and comparison platform that helps users explore flights based on price, safety ratings, estimated emissions, and overall environmental impact.

How is Flight Sight different from other flight websites?

Unlike traditional booking websites that focus mostly on cost and schedule, Flight Sight gives travelers additional information that supports more responsible and informed decisions, including safety insights and sustainability-focused metrics.

What are safety ratings based on?

Safety ratings are designed to help users compare airlines and routes using available safety-related information, such as historical performance, operational reliability, and other safety indicators presented through the platform.

How do you measure flight emissions?

Flight emissions are estimated using available trip and route data to give users a general idea of how much environmental impact a flight may have compared with other options.

Can I choose flights based on environmental impact?

Yes. Flight Sight is built to help users compare flight options beyond price alone, including emissions and other environmental considerations, so you can make choices that better match your priorities.

Can I still find affordable flights on Flight Sight?

Absolutely. Flight Sight allows users to compare prices while also considering other important factors like safety and sustainability, making it easier to balance budget and values.

Does Flight Sight only show eco-friendly flights?

No. Flight Sight shows a range of flight options, but it provides added information that helps users identify which options may have lower emissions or a smaller environmental footprint.

Who is Flight Sight for?

Flight Sight is for anyone who wants a more complete view of their travel options, especially travelers who care about safety, sustainability, and making better-informed booking decisions.

Can I save flights and come back later?

Yes. Flight Sight includes features that let users save flights and revisit them later when comparing options or finalizing travel plans.

How can I contact the Flight Sight team?

The main way to reach the Flight Sight team is through the project GitHub page, where you can explore the project and connect with the team for questions, feedback, or collaboration.
