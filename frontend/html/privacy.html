Privacy Policy

At Flight Sight, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, how that information may be used, and the choices you have while using our platform.

Your privacy matters

Flight Sight is designed to help users compare and book flights using insights like pricing, safety ratings, emissions, and environmental impact. We aim to handle user information responsibly and transparently.

1. Information We May Collect

When you use Flight Sight, we may collect information that helps us provide and improve the platform. This can include account details, saved flight preferences, search activity, booking-related selections, and general usage information.

  • Name or account-related information you provide
  • Flight search details such as departure, destination, and travel dates
  • Saved flights, alerts, and user preferences
  • Basic technical information such as browser or device type

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate Flight Sight, personalize the user experience, support core booking features, and improve how the platform presents pricing, safety, and sustainability insights.

  • To display relevant flight results and comparisons
  • To save flights and support user account features
  • To improve website performance and usability
  • To communicate updates or respond to questions and feedback

3. Safety, Emissions, and Environmental Data

Flight Sight may present estimated safety ratings, emissions information, and environmental impact insights to help users make better-informed travel choices. These values are intended to support comparison and decision-making within the platform.

4. Sharing of Information

Flight Sight does not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the platform, maintain services, comply with legal obligations, or support essential website functionality.

5. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect user information. While no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, Flight Sight seeks to take practical steps to reduce risk and protect data.

6. Cookies and Site Usage

Flight Sight may use cookies or similar technologies to support login sessions, remember user preferences, and better understand how visitors interact with the site.

7. Your Choices

Users may choose what information they provide through the platform. Depending on the site features available, users may also update saved preferences, remove saved flights, or reach out with privacy-related questions.

8. Third-Party Links

Some parts of Flight Sight may link to third-party resources, such as GitHub or other external services. Flight Sight is not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time to reflect changes to the platform or its features. Any updates will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about Flight Sight in general, please visit our GitHub page: github.com/ZaryaA101/FlightSight
